How MarketMind works

Observe. Connect. Detect. Reason. Personalize. Explain.

The goal is not to produce a magical buy/sell answer. The goal is to compress a large amount of evidence into an inspectable chain of reasoning that helps a human make a better-informed decision.
01 · OBSERVE

Collect evidence

Financials, prices, news, filings, management commentary and portfolio data.

02 · CONNECT

Map relationships

Companies, sectors, commodities, currencies, suppliers and historical analogues.

03 · DETECT

Find divergence

Thesis decay, accounting conflict, hidden concentration and repeating risk patterns.

04 · REASON

Build causal chains

Explainable paths with direction, magnitude, evidence and counter-thesis checks.

05 · PERSONALIZE

Map to the investor

Portfolio, watchlist, original thesis, previous decisions and risk concentration.

06 · EXPLAIN

Surface only what matters

Evidence-linked alerts, reports, visual reasoning and natural-language voice answers.
