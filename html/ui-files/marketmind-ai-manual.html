MarketMind AI: Quantitative Equity Platform & Autonomous Voice Copilot

An institutional-grade stock intelligence platform designed for 270+ National Stock Exchange (NSE) equities, researched with AI assistance to replace retail emotional guessing with real-time order book imbalances, causal supply chain ripple effects, and hands-free voice control.

The Retail Problem (Why 90% Lose Money)

According to SEBI data, over 90% of retail traders lose capital. They trade based on delayed news, WhatsApp rumors, and lagging indicators (RSI/MACD). When a trade goes into loss, they hold onto it with "hope" until wiped out.

The AI Institutional Solution

MarketMind AI operates like institutional quant hedge funds. It measures live buyer vs. seller queue depth, true institutional average prices, economic domino reactions, and mathematically enforced stop-loss exit points.

The 5 Core Intelligence Engines (Explained Simply — No Formulas) FastAPI Backend
1. Directional Probability Model (recommendations_service.py) Buyer vs. Seller Strength
What It Does: Calculates the exact percentage chance (e.g. 66% Upward Probability) that a stock will continue rising today, instead of giving a subjective guess.
How It Works: Evaluates 3 real-world clues: (1) Are there more active limit buyers than sellers in the exchange order queue? (2) Is the price trading above today's institutional average price (VWAP)? (3) Is trading volume unusually heavy? It turns these 3 signals into a clear 0% to 100% confidence score.
Dalal Street Example: Syrma SGS has huge buyer demand at ₹1,635 with double normal volume. The engine computes a 66.5% probability of upward continuation and issues a STRONG BUY signal targeting ₹1,729.00.
2. Macro Domino Shock Engine (domino_service.py) Supply Chain Ripple Effects
What It Does: Tracks how a change in global commodities (Crude Oil, Gold) or RBI interest rates cascades step-by-step through company supply chains and profit margins.
How It Works: When raw material prices spike, it automatically traces who suffers higher costs and who makes higher profits across connected corporate sectors.
Dalal Street Example: If Crude Oil jumps +20%, IndiGo Airlines loses profits because aviation fuel becomes expensive, while ONGC gains +₹1,120 Crore because it drills and sells crude oil at higher prices.
3. Stock DNA Behavioral Twin Finder (dna_service.py) Smart Stock Matchmaker
What It Does: Finds "twin" stocks that share the same financial DNA (growth, valuation, debt, and momentum) so you never have to chase an overpriced stock.
How It Works: Analyzes 8 fundamental and technical characteristics across 270 NSE companies to detect stocks that behave almost identically in the market.
Dalal Street Example: When Tata Motors rallies +35% and becomes too risky to buy, Stock DNA scans 270 stocks and points you to Bharat Forge (89% twin match), allowing you to capture the auto boom at a safer price.
4. 1-Day Monte Carlo Risk Gauge portfolio_service.py
What It Does: Simulates 1,000 possible market days to tell you the maximum realistic loss on your portfolio in plain Rupees.
How It Works: Uses 1 year of real historical price swings to stress-test your holdings across 1,000 randomized scenarios.
Example: For a ₹10 Lakh portfolio in Reliance and Adani, it certifies: "On 95 out of 100 days, your maximum 1-day loss will not exceed ₹18,450."
5. Smart Voice Phonetic Matcher voice_service.py
What It Does: Accurately identifies stock names from voice commands, even with heavy Indian accents, background noise, or voice typos.
How It Works: Compares spoken syllable patterns against 270 official NSE company names to find the closest match instantly.
Example: Spoken query "adacni entirerpice" immediately matches ticker ADANIENT, opening the stock card and chart on screen hands-free.

Evaluator Viva Defense: Top 7 Technical & Conceptual Questions Interview Q&A
Q1: "Why did you use explainable quantitative logic instead of a Deep Learning Neural Network (LSTM)?"
Defense: Deep learning neural networks are "black boxes" that overfit to market noise and hallucinate unpredictable outputs. Institutional hedge funds like Citadel and Renaissance Technologies do not let black-box neural networks make live trading decisions because their risks cannot be audited. Our 5 quantitative engines provide 100% explainable, deterministic logic with sub-5 millisecond response times and zero hallucination risk.
Q2: "What is the exact role of Gemini AI in MarketMind, and what happens if Gemini is offline?"
Defense: Gemini does not calculate numbers, stock targets, or risk percentages—all math is computed by our fast Python backend. Gemini's role is an Institutional Voice Synthesizer: it takes our calculated numbers and formats them into a polite, concise 35-word spoken response. If Gemini goes offline or lags past 2.2 seconds, our backend instantly uses an offline fallback template that speaks the exact price, target, and stop loss without delay.
Q3: "How does the voice system understand misspelled stock names or Indian accents?"
Defense: It removes conversational filler words ("show me", "share price"), breaks the audio into phonetic pieces, and compares the character sequence against 270 official company names and common aliases. If similarity is above 80%, it immediately matches the stock (e.g., "adacni entirerpice" → ADANIENT).
Q4: "How does Acoustic Echo Cancellation prevent Alex from talking to himself through the mic?"
Defense: When Alex speaks through the laptop speakers, the mic could pick up his own voice and treat it as a new user command. We track an active audio flag (isPlayingAudioRef). If incoming sound matches what Alex is currently speaking, the audio input is blocked. We also enforce a 450ms silence pause after speaking before re-opening the mic.
Q5: "How does the platform handle multi-turn conversations (pronouns like 'it')?"
Defense: The client maintains a rolling memory of recent conversation turns. If a user first asks "How is Reliance doing?" and then follows up with "What is its stop loss?", the backend checks the history stack, identifies that RELIANCE was the active subject, and answers for Reliance without forcing the user to repeat the company name.
Q6: "How does MarketMind handle post-market hours (after 3:30 PM IST)?"
Defense: The platform tracks official Indian Market Hours (09:15 to 15:30 IST, Monday through Friday). After 3:30 PM, it automatically switches to POST-MARKET / CLOSED status, freezes prices to official end-of-day settlement rates, and displays preparation analytics for the next morning's market opening.
Q7: "What regulatory safety guardrails prevent reckless financial advice?"
Defense: If a user asks speculative questions like "How can I double my money in 1 week?" or "Should I take a loan to trade?", strict SEBI prudence rules intercept the query. Alex politely refuses, warns against borrowed capital, and clarifies that MarketMind is an educational quantitative platform with zero guaranteed profits.
