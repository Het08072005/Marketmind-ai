1. P&L (Profit & Loss) — Ye Kya Hota Hai?

P&L (Profit and Loss) batata hai ki kisi news ya regulatory event se company ke net munafey ya nuksan me kitne crore rupees ka direct financial change aayega.

2. Float Yield — Ye Kya Hota Hai?

Float Yield bank ke paas temporarily jama idle cash par milne wali interest income hoti hai. FPI compliance easy hone se bank custody deposits par extra risk-free return kama leti.

3. EBITDA — Ye Kya Hota Hai?

EBITDA company ke core business operations ka actual munafa hota hai. Tax aur interest hatane se pehle ka yeh number batata hai ki main dhandha kitna cash kama raha.

4. NIM (Net Interest Margin) — Ye Kya Hai?

NIM bank ka main interest munafa hota hai. Loans par kamaye interest aur deposit par diye gaye interest ke beech ka difference bank ka net margin banata hai.

5. FX Realization — Ye Kya Hota Hai?

FX Realization dollar aur rupee ke currency conversion ka munafa hota hai. IT company videsh se dollar kamati hai, isliye exchange rate badalne se rupee earning directly badalti hai.

6. Price Reaction (Accumulating) — Ye Kya Hai?

Stock Price Reaction batata hai ki news aane par buyers ya sellers active hue. Accumulating ka matlab institutional khareedari hai, jabki Consolidating ka matlab price range me sthir rehna hai.
